# 10. Product Roadmap

## 0. V2 Upgrade Plan (Phase 3)
For technical dependencies, risks, and implementation details of immediate tasks, see the [V2 Upgrade Plan](./Audits/03_V2_UPGRADE_PLAN.md).

## Horizon 1: The Agnostic Foundation (Current)
- **Nationwide Support**: Dynamic Universities, TVETs, and Private Colleges.
- **Find My Res V3**: Map view, advanced filters, institution-linked campuses.
- **Referral V2**: Professional affiliate dashboard and automated conversion tracking.
- **Residence Portal v1**: Application management and handover exports.

## Horizon 2: The Enterprise Expansion (Short-term)
- **CRM Integration**: Centralized student support and follow-up logging.
- **Parent Portal**: View-only access for parents to track child's application.
- **Institution Dashboards**: Direct portals for Universities/TVETs to view accredited housing stats.
- **Maintenance & Lease**: Digital lease signing and maintenance ticket management in Residence Portal.

## Horizon 3: The Digital Ecosystem (Long-term)
- **Mobile Apps**: Native iOS and Android applications for students and landlords.
- **ResBot AI v2**: Predictive vacancy analytics and smart accommodation recommendations.
- **Fintech Integration**: Automated payment distribution and rent collection.
- **API Marketplace**: Open APIs for third-party student service integrations.
