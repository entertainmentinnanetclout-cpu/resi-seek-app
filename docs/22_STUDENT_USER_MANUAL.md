# 22. Student User Manual

## 1. Finding Your Home
- Use the **Audience Selector** (University / TVET / Private) to narrow down listings that match your funding type.
- Click the **Favorite (Heart)** icon to save residences for comparison.

## 2. The Application Process
1. **Choose**: Click Apply on your preferred residence.
2. **Setup**: Complete your profile (Campus, Course, etc.).
3. **Upload**: Attach your ID, Student Photo, and NSFAS/Funding proof.
4. **Track**: Check your Dashboard for status updates.

## 3. Refer & Earn
- Copy your unique code from the **Referrals** page.
- Share it with friends.
- When they sign up, you earn a signup bonus. When they pay for services, you earn a commission.
